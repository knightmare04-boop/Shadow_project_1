Shadow Graph Replay

Watch a fraud-detection system at work — 160 real bank transactions, replayed exactly as the system processed them

Every dot is an account; every arrow is a payment. Press Run and the payment network builds itself, day by day. Payments older than a week fade away. When a new payment completes a loop (money returning to where it started) or too many accounts pay into one place, the system notices, gives the payment a risk score, and raises an alarm — with its reasons attached. Tip: pick a slow speed, or use Step to follow one payment at a time.
